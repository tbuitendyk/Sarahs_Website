June 20 2025 Update

June 20th… I can’t believe that we are already in June! And June 20th?! Already?

I have some very, very exciting news!

In April I wrote two short stories for an Anthology challenge and submitted them. I waited for what seemed like forever but actually wasn’t and then I got an email saying that one of my stories was accepted! The funny thing is it was my least favorite of the two, and also the least favorite of my mom and sister (Who helped go over it for me.)

I don’t know when the Anthology will be published (The lady in charge said she hoped sometime this year) but I am so excited that I will actually be a published author!

The nice thing about the Anthology is that all the proceeds will be going out for people who can’t get Bibles for themselves! The Anthology is called Light in the Darkness. And when I get more info I’ll put it here on the website.

My Rise of the Dragons is going quite well. I am basically starting over because it didn’t have the depth that I wanted in the story. But it is honestly much better now. I feel like it has more depth, more interesting characters and more everything.

I’m planning on the story to be roughly 75,000 words, and right now I am at 10,000. I know that that is not much compared to how many words I want, but I figured if I do about 10,000 words a week (2,000 words in five days) I can get it all written out in two months or so. It might be a bit longer, because for the past couple weeks I have been busy and haven’t written anything.

Not too bad.

I am truly enjoying writing this story.

On the other works that are being written I have decided that I would focus on a single work for a few months to finish it, and then move onto the next story.
