Anthology · Books for Bibles Press

Light Through His Love

“And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.”

John 1:5

Books for Bibles Press presents its first anthology, featuring how God’s love shines even in the darkest places. Ten young authors showcase their talents in thought provoking stories, splashing the pages with rays of light.

Stories include

  • Realizing God wants everyone to be His child, no matter their circumstances
  • A young woman discovering what her ministry is
  • Rediscovering one’s passion in a very unique way
  • A Bible character discovering what it means to follow God
  • Traveling to another country and the hardships of learning to become a missionary
  • Showing someone God’s love even when it is difficult
  • A journey from darkness to peace and forgiveness
  • Learning how God’s love guides one’s footsteps through the hills and valleys of life
  • Bringing a ray of sunshine to injured soldiers
  • A ride on the Titanic
Buy the book on Amazon

Books for Bibles is an organization dedicated to selling books to buy Bibles for those who might not have easy access to them. When you buy a book, you give a Bible! We have yearly anthologies on a multitude of topics in which any aspiring author may participate. To find out more, please check out our website at booksforbibles.wordpress.com.
