Words of Love

Words of Love Sarah Buitendyk

Words of Love is a Biblical Fiction Fantasy story.

Barra is a mother in desperate need of help. Her daughter has been plagued by demons for years, and she and her husband have spent all they can to try and help their daughter.

They have lost hope. Barra is living in darkness and despair.

It isn’t until Barra meets a mysterious man named Isoa that she begins to believe, and when she begins to see light.

Barra is the woman who goes to Jesus, who’s daughter was healed.

Content Labels – The daughter is demon possessed, but it’s not described too much.
