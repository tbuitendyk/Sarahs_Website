About Me

Hi!

I’m Sarah Buitendyk, a young author who writes clean, contemporary, suspense, and fantasy with romance woven into the stories. My books feature Epic Adventures, close friendships, and common people thrust into uncommon situations. I hope you enjoy your visit to this little corner of the Internet that is mine.

I have ideas floating around my head all the time, and have a whole document dedicated to the random ideas that may or may not turn into full blown series in the future. That is essentially what happened with a whole overarching world idea that I have. It all started with a prompt: You are in an elevator and strangers get in with you. One of them hits the emergency stop button and turns to you saying “I bet you’re wondering what is going on.”

A little more about who I am

I live in Mexico with my family on a small ‘farm’ and have some chickens, a dog, two cats, and (in the future) some sheep. Being in the country is, honestly, the place that I am the happiest. I love the fresh air, the birds, the general quiet. The chance to enjoy being outside without having concrete and cars all around.

When I’m not writing (or learning about writing, or practicing editing) I usually am reading, doing any number of crafts, or trying to glean every last scrap of information on the latest thing that has caught my attention. (be it a Youtube channel, a movie or tv series, or some random thing).

My strongest passion though is following God, learning about him, and growing to walk in his ways.

This defines me.

If you would like to see my book reviews go to Reader’s Cove (scbbooks0.wordpress.com).
